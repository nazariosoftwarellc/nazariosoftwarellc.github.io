### How does the free trial work?

Better Times is free to use for 14 days. After that, it costs $5 USD. No subscriptions or ads.

### How do I buy the extension?

Click the Better Times icon in your browser's toolbar, then click "Purchase Better Times."

#### Chrome & Firefox

On Chrome & Firefox, the purchase link will open the extension settings in a new tab. Click the "Purchase" link to buy the app from Stripe. Note the email address used during your purchase.

When the purchase is finished, go back to the extension settings page (the one with the "Purchase" button). Enter your email used while buying and click "Restore Purchase." The app should unlock! [Email me][email] with any issues.

#### Safari

On Safari, the purchase link will open the Better Times app. An in-app purchase there unlocks the app.

#### Cross-purchase

If you buy the app on Chrome or Firefox, you unlock the:

- Chrome version
- Firefox version

If you buy the Safari version, you unlock the:

- Safari version
- Chrome version
- Firefox version

(This is due to App Store rules).
